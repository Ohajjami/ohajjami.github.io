I study how human resource development can support human-centered leadership, learning, and capacity development while responding to evolving organizational and technological contexts.

Research Interests

Leadership Development and Identity Negotiation in Complex Organizational Contexts

This research stream examines how leaders develop and negotiate their identities through sensemaking of challenging experiences, competing expectations, and social interactions. It explores how institutional, cultural, and relational contexts shape leaders' understanding and enactment of who they are as leaders. The stream positions HRD as a key mechanism for supporting identity development, integration, and effective leadership enactment.

Technology-Enabled Human Resource Development and Workplace Learning

This research stream examines how emerging technologies, the metaverse, generative AI, digital microcredentials, and physical AI, are reshaping workplace and higher education learning, and what HRD's role is in that transformation. It studies institutional readiness for adopting these technologies and the ecosystem conditions that enable or constrain their value, extending to whether technology-mediated relationships can substitute for or support the human relational work, empathy, mentoring, leadership development, that HRD has traditionally depended on people to provide.
